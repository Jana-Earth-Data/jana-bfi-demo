# 05 — Nepal sector-ESRM sources hunt

Research date: 2026-07-30
Author: Claude (Option E research pass, R5)

---

## 1. Executive summary

**No authoritative Nepal-authored source containing sector-specific ESRM Q&A checklists (in the style of our 25 sector supplement questions across hydropower, cement, textiles, steel, chemicals, brick, agriculture) currently exists.** Confidence: **HIGH** for hydropower/cement/textiles/agriculture; **MEDIUM-HIGH** for brick/steel/chemicals.

What Nepal actually has, in order of authority:

1. **NRB ESRM Guideline (Feb 2022)** — 13-question **generic** ESDD checklist (Annex 5) that applies to all sectors uniformly. Sector nuance appears only in the *guidance notes* (which name-drop cement/brick/textile/steel/agriculture as illustrative examples of pollution risks) and in **Annex 2**, which is a **hydropower-specific documentation matrix**, not a Q&A.
2. **NBA "Implementation Handbook on ESRM for Banks and Financial Institutions in Nepal" (Feb 20, 2026)** — recently released, positioned as operational guidance *between* the NRB Guideline and day-to-day banking; press description talks about transaction-level screening/assessment process, not sector-specific Q&A. PDF is not publicly downloadable from the NBA site as of research date and would need to be requested from NBA directly to confirm.
3. **Nepal Green Finance Taxonomy (Oct 2024)** — sector eligibility criteria (Green/Amber/Red) for a defined set of activities. This is *taxonomy classification*, not ESRM Q&A; we already ingest it via the taxonomy wizard.
4. **Nepal Environment Protection Rules 2020, Schedules 1/2/3** — government EIA/IEE/BES documentation thresholds by industry, not bank-facing questions.

Recommendation: **fall back to IFC EHS Guidelines** as the anchor for the 25 sector-supplement questions, with a **brick-sector gap** flagged. Detail in section 5.

## 3. Candidate sources found

### 3.1 NRB ESRM Guideline (Feb 2022) — Annex 5 ESDD Checklist

- **Title:** Guideline on Environmental & Social Risk Management (ESRM) for Banks and Financial Institutions
- **Issuer:** Nepal Rastra Bank, Banks and Financial Institutions Regulation Department
- **Date:** February 2022
- **URL:** <https://www.nrb.org.np/contents/uploads/2022/02/Final-ESRM-with-cover.pdf>
- **Authoritative or advisory:** **Authoritative** — mandatory for all Class A-D BFIs.
- **Sectors covered by sector-specific Q&A:** **NONE.** Annex 5 is a single, sector-agnostic 13-question checklist (1.1–1.4 General, 2.1–2.5 Environmental H&S, 3.1–3.4 Social). Sector nuance appears only in the guidance notes' illustrative examples ("Best potential for energy efficiency improvements are in sectors operating in high energy-consuming industries. e.g. steel, cement, textile & dyeing, paper, rubber, plastic, beverage, sugar" — verbatim from p.38 guidance note 2.4). The Guideline *lists* 10 "critical sectors" (RMG washing/dyeing, small steel re-rolling, brick kilns, tanning, pesticides, chemicals, rubber/plastic, batteries, paper, foundries) but only to determine *which small loans get pushed through the generic checklist*, not to define sector-specific questions.
- **Format:** Multiple-choice a/b/c/d per question with a/b/c mapped to Low/Medium/High risk, plus a "Not applicable" and free-text Remarks. Assessment is generic and reused across every sector.
- **Assessment:** Cannot replace our sector supplements — it does not carry sector-specific content. Already fully ingested by the demo (task #6 "Phase 2: ESDD wizard, Annex 5 verbatim capture").

### 3.2 NRB ESRM Guideline — Annex 2 (Hydropower)

- **Title:** Annex 2 — E&S Risk Management Considerations in Hydropower Projects (inside the same 2022 Guideline PDF)
- **Format:** Two tables — (i) a **documentation matrix** keyed by capacity band (>50 MW: EIA required; 1–50 MW: IEE required; <1 MW: none / local licensing), listing the documents the bank must collect (company registration, survey license, EIA/IEE approval, development license, PPA); and (ii) a **"Typical E&S aspects" checklist** for hydropower listing parameters to look for (streamflow up/downstream, water quality, sediment transport, dam CO2/H2S/CH4 emissions, aquatic/terrestrial ecology, resettlement, IPs). This is a **reference matrix**, not a scored Q&A.
- **Authoritative:** Yes, part of the mandatory NRB Guideline.
- **Assessment:** Could supplement the hydropower supplement with the documentation matrix (as a document-checklist widget) and the E&S parameter list. Already flagged in the Circular 22 breakdown research file (`02-circular-22-authoritative.md`). Not enough on its own to replace the 3–4 hydropower sector questions we currently generate — it is document/parameter checkboxes, not risk questions.

### 3.3 NBA "Implementation Handbook on ESRM for Banks and Financial Institutions in Nepal" (Feb 20, 2026)

- **Title:** Implementation Handbook on Environmental and Social Risk Management (ESRM) for Banks and Financial Institutions in Nepal
- **Issuer:** Nepal Bankers' Association
- **Date:** February 20, 2026 (release announcement dated in NBA news)
- **URL of announcement:** <https://nepalbankers.com.np/nba-released-esrm-implementation-handbook/> (no direct PDF link)
- **Authoritative or advisory:** **Advisory** (industry best practice from banking association), aligned with NRB regulatory requirements. Not a regulatory instrument but likely to be adopted as de-facto practice by NBA member banks (including Laxmi Sunrise).
- **Sectors covered:** **Unknown from public sources.** The public description talks about transaction-level screening, assessment, management, monitoring — process guidance, not sector Q&A. It also references drawing on IFC Performance Standards, OECD Guidelines, and Equator Principles. Given the framing ("bridge the gap between NRB's ESRM Guideline and day-to-day banking operations"), it is more likely to be process/workflow guidance than a sector questionnaire, but this cannot be confirmed without the PDF.
- **Format:** Unknown (PDF not obtainable through the NBA site).
- **Access flag for the user:** Ask Laxmi Sunrise's ESRM team to share their NBA Implementation Handbook copy. If it does contain sector questions, we should re-run this research pass against the handbook. If it is process/workflow guidance (most likely), the recommendation below stands.
- **Assessment:** Cannot assess until the PDF is obtained. **This is the single biggest residual uncertainty in this research pass.** Recommended action: request from Laxmi Sunrise or email NBA (<nba@nepalbankers.com.np>).

## 5. IFC EHS Guidelines fallback plan — pre-flight

The IFC EHS Guidelines are the World Bank Group's technical reference documents for Good International Industry Practice under Performance Standard 3. They are *explicitly referenced* by the NRB 2022 ESRM Guideline as best-practice standards (see Guideline §6, "Adherence to IFC Performance Standards will be considered as good practice"). Citing them for the sector supplements is defensible and aligned with the primary Nepal regulator's stated position.

### 5.1 Coverage per sector (7 sectors)

| Sector | IFC EHS Guideline | Publication date | URL | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hydropower** | Good Practice Note: Environmental, Health, and Safety Approaches for Hydropower Projects | March 2018 | <https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2018/publications-gpn-ehshydropwer> | **Strong** — 200+ page GPN. Covers RoR diversion, RoR reservoir, storage reservoir, pumped storage. Anchor supplement questions here. |
| **Cement** | EHS Guidelines for Cement and Lime Manufacturing | 2022 (updated) | <https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2022/2022-cement-lime-manufacturing-ehs-guidelines-en.pdf> | **Strong** — energy, GHG, air (PM, NOx, SOx), wastewater, solid waste. Directly maps to our cement supplement. |
| **Textiles** | EHS Guidelines for Textile Manufacturing | 2007 (current) | <https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/general-environmental-health-and-safety-guidelines/ehs-guidelines-general-manufacturing> (linked from the general-manufacturing hub) | **Strong** — natural, synthetic, regenerated fibers; dyeing/finishing water and chemical impacts; direct match for our textile supplement. |
| **Steel** | EHS Guidelines for Integrated Steel Mills **and** Foundries (both apply — Nepal has small re-rolling mills which are closer to Foundries scope) | 2007 (both) | Steel: <https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2000/2007-integrated-steel-mills-ehs-guidelines-en.pdf>; Foundries: <https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2000/2007-foundries-ehs-guidelines-en.pdf> | **Strong for large mills, adequate for small re-rolling** — use Foundries for the small re-rolling case which matches Nepal reality. |
| **Chemicals** | EHS Guidelines for Bulk Chemical Terminals + Pesticides Manufacturing/Formulation + Coal Processing (choose per sub-sector); General EHS Guidelines cover cross-cutting | 2007 (various) | Hub: <https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/general-environmental-health-and-safety-guidelines/ehs-guidelines-chemicals> | **Strong** — pick the sub-sector guideline that matches the borrower's activity (agrochemical, formulator, terminal, etc.). |
| **Brick** | **GAP — no dedicated IFC EHS Guideline for brick manufacturing.** Nearest fits: (a) General EHS Guidelines; (b) EHS Guidelines for Ceramic Tile & Sanitary Ware Manufacturing; (c) Construction Materials Extraction (for clay quarrying); (d) Nepal-specific MinErgy/ICIMOD Brick Sector National Policy Framework (2017) as a Nepal-context reference | Various | General EHS Guidelines: <https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/general-environmental-health-and-safety-guidelines> | **Thin — flag for the user.** Cite General EHS Guidelines + MinErgy/ICIMOD 2017 as the composite anchor. |
| **Agriculture** | EHS Guidelines for Annual Crop Production **and** Perennial Crop Production | 2016 (both) | Annual: <https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2010/2016-annual-crop-production-ehs-guidelines-en.pdf>; Perennial: <https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2010/2016-perennial-crop-production-ehs-guidelines-en.pdf> | **Strong** — pesticide, fertilizer, water use, worker safety, land tenure, biodiversity. Direct match. |

### 5.2 Sectors where coverage is thin

- **Brick** — no dedicated IFC EHS Guideline. Recommended composite anchor: General EHS Guidelines (air, waste, OHS sections) + MinErgy/ICIMOD Nepal Brick Sector Policy Framework (2017) for the Nepal-specific kiln-type, migrant-labor, and Kathmandu-Valley air-quality color. This is a defensible middle path but the user should be told the citation stack is thinner than for the other six sectors.
- **Chemicals** — coverage is strong but *fragmented* across sub-sectors. The tool needs to pick the right sub-sector guideline per borrower (pesticides-manufacturing vs. formulator vs. terminal vs. bulk chemicals). A single generic "chemicals" citation would be sloppy.

### 5.3 Citation footer template (recommended)

For each sector supplement question, add a footnote/citation of the form:

> Basis: IFC EHS Guidelines for [Sector], §[Section] (WBG, [Year]). Aligned with NRB ESRM Guideline §6 (Feb 2022), which recognizes IFC Performance Standards as good practice. Nepal-specific context: [MoFE EIA Manual / MinErgy Brick Framework / Nepal EPR 2020 Schedule X — where applicable].

This gives Laxmi Sunrise's compliance team a defensible answer to "where did this question come from?" — international best practice explicitly endorsed by their own regulator, with Nepal-specific overlays where they exist.
