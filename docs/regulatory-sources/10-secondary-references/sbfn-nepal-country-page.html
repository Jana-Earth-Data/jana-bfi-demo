Timeline

  • 2024
    Framework Development

    • NRB publishes the Nepal Green Finance Taxonomy

  • 2023
    Framework Development

    • NRB updated the Stress Testing Guidelines in 2023

    Research and Engagement

    • The Government of Nepal and 16 development partners prioritize investment in Green, Resilient, and Inclusive Development

  • 2022
    Framework Development

    • NRB updates the Guideline on Environmental and Social Risk Management (ESRM) for Banks and Financial Institutions (BFIs) to include consideration of climate-related risks

  • 2020
    Framework Development

    • NRB issues Financial Literacy Framework issued to promote financial education and inclusion

    • Government of Nepal issues Environmental Protection Rules, 2020 (2077)

    • NRB Unified Directive mandates all banks and financial institutions to follow the Guideline on Environmental & Social Risk Management (ESRM)

  • 2019
    Framework Development

    • Government of Nepal passes the Environmental Protection Act, 2019 (2076), which creates the Environment Protection Fund

    Research and Engagement

    • NRB conducts survey on awareness of sustainable banking practices among BFIs in October

  • 2018
    Framework Development

    • NRB issues Environmental and Social Due Diligence (ESDD) Checklist and E&S Risk Rating Tool and Annual Reporting Template

    • NRB issues Guideline on Environmental & Social Risk Management (ESRM) for Banks and Financial Institutions

  • 2017
    Framework Development

    • National Planning Commission issues SDGs Status and Roadmap: 2016 – 2030 (Government of Nepal)

    • National Planning Commission publishes SDGs, Baseline Report (Government of Nepal)

    • National Planning Commission publishes National Review of SDGs (Government of Nepal)

  • 2016
    Framework Development

    • National Planning Commission publishes Sustainable Development Goals Preliminary Report: 2016 – 2030 (Government of Nepal)

    • Ministry of Population and Environment sets Nepal’s Intended Nationally Determined Contributions (Government of Nepal)

  • 2014
    Research and Engagement

    • Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) joins SBFN
